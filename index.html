Earn Eternal Rewards Even After Death

The Prophet Muhammad (ﷺ) said: "When a man dies, his deeds come to an end except for three things: Sadaqah Jariyah (ceaseless charity); a knowledge which is beneficial or a virtuous descendant who prays for him."

What is Sadaqah Jariyah?

Sadaqah Jariyah means ongoing charity, continuous charity, ceaseless charity a good deed whose reward keeps flowing even after death. Unlike a one-time donation, whose benefit ends when it is spent, ongoing charity creates a lasting benefit: a well keeps giving water, a Mosque keeps hosting prayers, a tree keeps giving shade and fruit and knowledge keeps being passed on. Sadaqah Jariyah is the best-known form of ongoing charity in Islam also called continuous charity in Islam. Also spelled sadaqa jariya (sometimes sadaqa jariyah, sadaqatul jariyah or sadaqah jariya) it means the same thing.

“When a person dies, their deeds come to an end except for three things: ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge or a righteous child who prays for them.” Sahih Muslim, Hadith 1631

“The parable of those who spend their wealth in the way of Allah is as the parable of a grain growing seven ears, in every ear a hundred grains.” Surah Al-Baqarah (2:261)

The 3 deeds that continue after death

  1. Ongoing charity (Sadaqah Jariyah): Water, Mosque, Tree, School, Hospital.
  2. Beneficial knowledge: Knowledge you taught and spread, that others keep using and passing on.
  3. A righteous child who prays for you: A child raised well, who prays, does good and teaches others.

A second narration adds: a copy of the Quran left behind, a mosque built, a house built for travellers, a canal dug and charity given during life and health. Sunan Ibn Majah, Hadith 242

Sadaqah Jariyah vs one-time Sadaqah

One-time SadaqahSadaqah Jariyah

6 Acts of Continuous Charity

Invest in your hereafter by implementing these easy, highly-rewarding deeds today. These are 6 acts of Sadaqah Jariyah you can start today.

1. Gift a Holy Quran

Provide a copy of the Holy Quran to an individual, a school or a local mosque. Every single time a letter from it is recited, an immense spiritual reward is directly credited to your account and when the reader teaches someone else, that reward grows further. How to start: gift one Quran to a student, a new Muslim or a mosque library.

2. Donate a Wheelchair

Donate mobility equipment or medical resources to a hospital or clinic. Every single instance an ailing, sick or elderly person finds comfort and uses it, you gain persistent blessings. Medical sadaqah jariyah is often forgotten, yet it directly eases pain and that ease continues for every patient who uses the equipment after you.

3. Build a Mosque

Participate financially or physically in building or maintaining a mosque. You earn continuous rewards for every single prayer, prostration and remembrance of Allah made inside it. “Whoever builds a mosque for Allah, Allah will build for him a house in Paradise.” (Sahih al-Bukhari 450; Sahih Muslim 533) You do not need to fund an entire Mosque even a small contribution counts.

4. Provide Clean Water

Place a functioning water cooler, filtration plant or dig a water well in a crowded public space. Providing access to clean water is highly emphasized as one of the best forms of charity. “The best charity is giving water to drink.” (Musnad Ahmad 17058) A water well is also one of the best sadaqah jariyah gifts for deceased parents.

5. Plant a Tree

Plant a tree or a crop field. Every time a human seeker, a passing traveler, a bird or an animal consumes its fresh fruit or finds comfortable shade underneath it, you gain automated continuous reward. “No Muslim plants a tree from which a bird, person or animal eats, except that it is charity for them.” (Sahih Muslim 1552) It costs almost nothing, yet keeps giving for decades.

6. Spread Beneficial Knowledge

And the easiest method: Share this message with others. Simply spreading beneficial knowledge grants you equivalent rewards whenever an inspired individual acts upon any item listed here. “The best among you are those who learn the Quran and teach it.” (Sahih al-Bukhari 5027) Teaching a skill, a verse or a good habit all fall under beneficial knowledge.

More acts that also count as Sadaqah Jariyah

These are not part of the 6 acts above, but classical lists include them and they complete the picture:

  • Raise a righteous child: The third deed in Sahih Muslim 1631.
  • Build a house or shelter for travellers: From the Ibn Majah 242 list.
  • Build a school or hospital: Every student taught and every patient treated becomes part of your ongoing reward.
  • Sponsor an orphan's education: Education keeps benefiting the child, their family and their community.
  • Establish a waqf (Islamic endowment): A gift invested so its returns keep funding good projects, year after year.

Sadaqah Jariyah for Deceased Parents and Loved Ones

Can I give sadaqah jariyah on behalf of someone who has passed away? Yes and it is one of the most beautiful ways to honour deceased parents. When Sa'd ibn 'Ubadah asked the Prophet (ﷺ) about the best charity for his late mother, he was told that giving water is among the best. A man whose mother died suddenly was also told that the reward of charity given on her behalf would reach her. References: Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 1388; Sunan Abi Dawud, Hadith 1679.

Best sadaqah jariyah for the deceased, in practice:

  1. Identify a cause: Water, education, Mosque or a Tree that will keep benefiting people.
  2. Give it in their name: Make the intention that the reward reaches your parent or loved one.
  3. Choose something lasting: A well, Mosque contribution, a school or a tree, not a one-time gift.
  4. Invite family to join: Shared giving multiplies both the impact and the reward.

How to start today even with little or no money

Sadaqah Jariyah is not only about money. “The most beloved deeds to Allah are the most consistent, even if small.” Sahih al-Bukhari, Hadith 6465

  1. Pick one act from the 6 above the one you can actually repeat.
  2. Start small: plant one tree, share one useful lesson, gift one Quran or give a small monthly donation.
  3. Make it regular: a small monthly donation, a tree every year or knowledge shared every week beats a large deed done once and abandoned.
  4. Share this page: sharing beneficial knowledge is itself sadaqah jariyah and it costs nothing.
