About This Site

A simple, free guide to Sadaqah Jariyah — ongoing charity that keeps rewarding you even after death.

What is this site?

This site explains Sadaqah Jariyah in simple words, with hadith references, and lists 6 acts you can start today: gift a Holy Qur'an, donate a wheelchair, build a masjid, provide clean water, plant a tree, and spread beneficial knowledge.

What this site is not

This is an informational guide, not a donation collection site. It does not collect money, and it does not replace a scholar for personal religious questions. For giving, always use a trusted organisation or give directly in your own community.

Our message, in one line

Start with one act today — even sharing this page is Sadaqah Jariyah, because every person who acts on it becomes part of your ongoing reward.
