Sadaqah Jariyah for Deceased Parents

Can charity benefit your deceased parents? What the hadith say and practical ways to give in their name.

One of the most asked questions about Sadaqah Jariyah is also one of the most personal: can I give it on behalf of my deceased parents? The short answer, from the hadith literature, is yes charity given on behalf of a deceased person can benefit them.

A narration in Sahih al-Bukhari (1388) describes a man who asked about his mother, who had died suddenly. He asked whether charity on her behalf would benefit her and the answer was yes:

A man said to the Prophet (p.b.u.h), "My mother died suddenly and I thought that if she had lived she would have given alms. So, if I give alms now on her behalf, will she get the reward?" The Prophet (ﷺ) replied in the affirmative. Sahih al-Bukhari 1388

Another narration (Sunan Abi Dawud 1679) records Sa'd ibn Ubadah giving a water well on behalf of his mother. So giving sadaqah jariyah for parents for a deceased mother or a deceased father is an established practice and its reward reaches them. Water in fact is one of the most mentioned forms of this charity a hadith in Musnad Ahmad (17058) calls giving water the best charity.

Practical ways to give for deceased parents

  • Build or contribute to a water well in their name, so every drink from it carries reward to them.
  • Contribute to a masjid: Even a small share of a building that people pray in.
  • Give a Quran: That will be read or fund the teaching of Quran to others.
  • Plant a tree: That gives shade and fruit for years.
  • Support education: An orphan's schooling or beneficial knowledge shared in their name.

What else benefits the deceased?

Charity is one of three continuing deeds named in Sahih Muslim 1631: ongoing charity, beneficial knowledge and a righteous child who prays for them. Dua for deceased parents is itself a continuing act of love and it costs nothing.

If money is tight, start with dua then add whatever small charity you can. A small, sincere, consistent act is better than a large act done once and forgotten. The reward is with Allah and the intention giving in their name for their benefit is what makes it Sadaqah Jariyah for them.
